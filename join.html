SAY HELLO

A short message.
A clear starting point.

You do not need to arrive with a finished research plan. Tell us where your interests meet the team’s work.

Email the team ↗
  1. Introduce yourself

    Tell us your study program or background and what kind of contribution interests you.

  2. Point to a topic

    Mention a project, technical area or question you would like to explore. Links to something you have built are welcome.

  3. Tell us what you are looking for

    Team involvement, a student project, a thesis or research collaboration — include your approximate availability. For research enquiries, you may attach a short CV.
