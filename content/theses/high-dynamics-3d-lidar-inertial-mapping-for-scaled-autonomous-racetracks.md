## About

A 3D LiDAR lets the car capture elevation, banking and barriers that our 2D map cannot hold. Such maps feed 3D localization, raceline optimization and perception. Existing mapping tools are a good start, but racing brings fast rotations, vibration, limited onboard compute and repetitive track sections. The outcome is a reproducible mapping pipeline, consistent maps with a road-surface model, and a comparison of existing tools plus one improvement. Localizing in the finished map is a separate thesis.
