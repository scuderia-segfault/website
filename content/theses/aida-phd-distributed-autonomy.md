## About

AIDA (AI-Driven Distributed Autonomy, FWF) studies how small language models can support planning and coordination in safety-critical driving. Vehicles keep only unusual or safety-relevant episodes in a shared fleet memory and recall similar cases in unfamiliar situations. Evaluation uses simulation and our RoboRacer cars, possibly extending to full-scale vehicles. The position runs 30 months at 40 h/week; start to be confirmed. To apply, send a CV, a one-page motivation letter, transcripts and two academic referees to joinus\@trustcps.eu.
