Unit XIII starts with a Serpent SRX8GTE R, a 1:8 electric GT chassis with four-wheel drive.

## Chassis assembly

The workbench photo shows the chassis plate, centre drivetrain and suspension components during assembly. We use this stage to check how the mechanical parts fit together and where the electronics and mounts can sit.

![The Serpent chassis during assembly](../../uploads/uni13_build_chassis.jpg)

## Motor fit and drivetrain clearance

The close-up marks the area around the motor mount and the gear assembly. Before bench testing, we want to check the gear mesh, clearance for the motor leads and access to the mounting screws.

![Motor and drivetrain detail with the mounting area marked](../../uploads/blog_uni13_build_motor.jpg)
