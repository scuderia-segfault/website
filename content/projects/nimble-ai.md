Running computer vision on small endpoint chips is limited by energy, latency and silicon area.

NimbleAI takes ideas from biological eyes and brains and from 3D-stacked silicon to build a combined sensing-processing neuromorphic architecture. It senses only data with high information value and discards data once it is not useful. The project targets 100x performance per mW and 50x lower latency than CPU/GPU processing of frame-based video, power in the order of tens of mW and about 50 mm² of silicon. TU Wien (Felix Resch, Radu Grosu) is a partner; the project ran from October 2022 to March 2026.
