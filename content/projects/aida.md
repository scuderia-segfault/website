Autonomous vehicles that share a road need models of their own dynamics and must coordinate with other vehicles. AIDA studies how learning can support both.

The FWF record lists vehicle dynamics, large language models, recurrent neural networks, distributed multi-agent systems and federated learning as the project's topics. The project is led by Agnes Poks at TU Wien, with Daniela Rus (MIT) as international participant. The plan runs from November 2026 to April 2029 under grant PAT3743226.

The work builds on our small-scale RoboRacer experiments and vehicle-dynamics models.
