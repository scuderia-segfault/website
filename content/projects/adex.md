Autonomous driving agents must be trained and tested on many traffic scenarios, and their behavior must meet requirements on safety, goals and comfort. Writing these scenarios and reward signals by hand is slow.

ADEX was funded by the FFG (project 880811) and led by Radu Grosu at TU Wien. Its outputs include MATS-Gym, which generates multi-agent traffic scenarios in the CARLA simulator from partial specifications; HPRS, which turns task specifications into shaped rewards; an algorithm for safe policy improvement in constrained Markov decision processes; and TD-Magic, which converts pictures of timing diagrams into formal specifications.
