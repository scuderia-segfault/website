TIMELINE

From Simulation to Our Own Lab

  1. 2020

    Virtual Grand Prix win

    First place at the IFAC Virtual Autonomous Grand Prix, raced entirely in simulation.

    Virtual / Berlin 2020: 1st place
  2. 2021

    Generation 1 debut

    Our first physical car, with a Traxxas chassis and NVIDIA Jetson, wins the 9th Grand Prix in Prague.

    Prague 2021: 1st place
  3. 2022

    Podium in Philadelphia

    Third place out of 38 teams at ICRA 2022.

    Philadelphia 2022: 3rd place
  4. 2023

    Last race for Generation 1

    Second place out of 41 teams at ICRA 2023 in London.

    London 2023: 2nd place
  5. 2024

    Generation 2 with Intel NUC

    Same chassis, new onboard computer. Fourth in Yokohama, second in Abu Dhabi and first place in Milano.

    Milano 2024: 1st place
  6. May 2025

    Our own lab

    Scuderia Segfault moves into the Autonomous Robots Lab of the Research Unit of Cyber-Physical Systems at TU Wien (Room PCEG13).

  7. 2026

    Hosting RoboRacer Vienna

    We host the 27th RoboRacer Autonomous Racing Competition at TU Wien.

    Vienna 2026: host
  8. Next

    Generation 3

    A new low chassis is in development in our lab.
