TIMELINE

From Simulation to Our Own Lab

  1. 2020

    Generation 1 and a virtual win

    Our first cars, with a Traxxas Fiesta chassis and Jetson TX2. The IFAC Grand Prix that year was raced in simulation, and we won it.

    Virtual / Berlin 2020: 1st place
  2. 2021

    First physical win

    Generation 1 wins the 9th Grand Prix in Prague.

    Prague 2021: 1st place
  3. 2022

    Generation 2

    New Traxxas Slash chassis and Jetson NX. Third place out of 38 teams in Philadelphia.

    Philadelphia 2022: 3rd place
  4. 2023

    Podium in London

    Second place out of 41 teams at ICRA 2023, the last race for Generation 2.

    London 2023: 2nd place
  5. 2024

    Generation 3 with Intel NUC

    Fourth in Yokohama, second in Abu Dhabi and first place in Milano.

    Milano 2024: 1st place
  6. June 2024

    Our own lab

    We got our own room before ICRA 2024 in Japan. After the renovation, we moved into the Autonomous Robots Lab of the Research Unit of Cyber-Physical Systems at TU Wien (Room PCEG13) in June 2024.

  7. March 2025

    Generation 4

    Back on the Fiesta chassis with an ASUS NUC 14 Pro.

  8. 2025

    Atlanta

    Fourth place at ICRA 2025 with Generation 4.

    Atlanta 2025: 4th place
  9. 2026

    Hosting RoboRacer Vienna

    We host the 27th RoboRacer Autonomous Racing Competition at TU Wien.

    Vienna 2026: host
  10. Next

    Generation 5

    A new low chassis is in development in our lab.
